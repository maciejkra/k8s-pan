Custom Prometheus alerts

apiVersion: monitoring.coreos.com/v1

kind: PrometheusRule

spec:

groups:

    name: app.alerts

      rules:

        alert: HighErrorRate

expr: |

sum(rate(http_requests_total{status=~"5.."}[5m])) > 0.05

for: 5m

annotations:

summary: "Error rate > 5% przez 5 min"

runbook_url: "https://runbooks.example.com/high-error"

AlertManager → Slack · PagerDuty · Opsgenie · email · webhook. Routing po severity, deduplication, silencing.
